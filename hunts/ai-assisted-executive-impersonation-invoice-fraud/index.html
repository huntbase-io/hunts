Brief

Context and Source. Microsoft recently detailed a campaign involving AI-assisted executive impersonation in their blog "Protecting organizations from AI-assisted executive impersonation and invoice fraud" (https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/09/10/protecting-organizations-ai-assisted-executive-impersonation-invoice-fraud/). This campaign is particularly dangerous because it bypasses traditional phishing filters using high-fidelity lures and lookalike domains. Our team has developed a hunt to identify the footprints of this activity. ## The Hypothesis. We hypothesize that an attacker is interacting with finance personnel through lookalike infrastructure like service-nowinc.com. Once the user is engaged, they are convinced to download and process a fraudulent invoice, typically involving a $50,000 ACH payment. ## Hunt Flow. The hunt begins by scoping. We use software inventory data to identify hosts with ServiceNow installed, as these are the most likely targets for this specific campaign. By narrowing our initial search, we reduce the noise from generic web traffic. Next, we pivot to network telemetry. We search DNS and HTTP logs for known lookalike domains. More importantly, we baseline all traffic containing the string "service-now" that does not go to the legitimate domain. We look for rare destinations visited by fewer than three hosts to catch infrastructure not yet indexed by threat intelligence. The final phase focuses on the filesystem. We look for the creation of PDF files containing "ServiceNow" in their names, specifically within user-writable directories like Downloads or Desktop. The correlation of a rare network connection followed by a file creation provides a high-confidence indicator of a successful lure. ## Blind Spots. There are two main limitations. First, if attackers use multi-hop proxy networks or Operational Relay Boxes (ORBs), the source IP and some session characteristics may be masked, appearing as generic traffic. Second, this hunt looks for the aftermath of an email. Without direct email body telemetry, we cannot see the specific AI markers until an analyst manually reviews the mailbox. ## How to Run. This hunt is formatted as a hunt.md playbook. It is designed to be imported into Huntbase or any runtime that supports the hunt.md specification. It provides a structured sequence of queries and triage steps to move from estate-wide telemetry to isolated victim endpoints.

# AI-Assisted Executive Impersonation and Invoice Fraud

This hunt identifies internal hosts interacting with lookalike infrastructure (e.g., service-nowinc.com) and the subsequent creation of fraudulent invoice PDF artifacts. It leverages the correlation between DNS resolutions, rare HTTP traffic to non-standard vendor domains, and file creation events in user-writable paths to isolate victims of high-fidelity Business Email Compromise (BEC).
