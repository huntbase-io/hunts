Identity Breach and Account Abuse

Medium severity TLP:CLEAR Research by ESET Research Published 14 Oct 2026

Hypothesis

Adversaries are leveraging corporate credentials exposed in third-party data breaches to bypass authentication, propagate phishing lures, and exfiltrate data from internal endpoints.

Brief

Why this hunt

ESET Research recently highlighted the ambiguity of data breach notices in What to do when a data breach notice tells you almost nothing. When a third-party service loses your data, the notice rarely explains if your corporate credentials are already being sold or used against your infrastructure. We developed this hunt to close that gap by turning external intelligence into a targeted internal investigation.

How the hunt flows

The hunt begins by identifying active managed devices through the hb_devices surface. This ensures the follow-on behavioral queries focus on systems currently reporting telemetry, reducing the noise from decommissioned or dormant assets.

Next, the hunt performs a parallel external intelligence check. It queries HaveIBeenPwned (HIBP) and Hudson Rock for any corporate email domains. This phase separates generic historical leaks from high-risk infostealer infections. An analyst or agent then weighs these results to create a list of high-risk identities for the next phase.

The investigation then pivots to internal behavioral surfaces. It examines hb_auth_signin to find successful sign-ins from rare source IPs or unusual geographic locations for the flagged accounts. This identifies successful authentication that may bypass traditional geo-fencing.

Simultaneously, the hunt scans hb_http_activity for web requests containing common phishing or credential-harvesting keywords like "verify" or "office". It also monitors hb_network_connection for outbound transfers exceeding 50MB originating from those same accounts. This three-surface approach helps verify if a compromised account is being used for internal lateral movement or data theft.

Finally, the hunt correlates the timing of the external breach with these internal anomalies. If an account appears in a fresh infostealer log and subsequently shows rare login patterns or high-volume data transfers, the hunt directs the analyst to revoke sessions and reset the identity.

Blind spots

This hunt has two primary limitations. First, without SSL decryption for HTTP traffic, the hunt can identify that a user visited a suspicious domain but cannot confirm if they submitted credentials on a specific URI path. Second, the hunt depends on high-fidelity authentication logs. If the environment lacks full MFA event details, distinguishing between a simple password replay and a sophisticated session token theft is difficult.

Running this hunt

This is a hunt.md playbook. It provides a structured flow that is more complex than a standard detection rule because it requires correlating external exposure with multi-surface behavior. You can import this file into Huntbase or any runtime that supports the hunt.md specification to automate the intelligence gathering and behavioral analysis phases.

Steps

  1. Query, scoping

    Scope active managed devices

    Identify recently active hosts to focus the internal behavioral queries.

    sql, reads hb_devices
    SELECT DISTINCT hostname AS device_hostnameFROM hb_devicesWHERE (lifecycle_state = 'active' OR lifecycle_state = 'running')  AND time >= datetime('now', '-{{lookback_days}} days')

    What a hit looks like. A list of hostnames that have reported inventory recently. Silence means no active devices are reporting.

  2. Query, enrichment

    Public data breach exposure

    Check if corporate accounts appear in public data breaches listed by HaveIBeenPwned.

    sql, reads hibp_breached_account
    SELECT account, name AS breach_name, data_classesFROM hibp_breached_accountWHERE instr(',' || '{{corporate_domains}}' || ',', ',' || LOWER(SUBSTR(account, INSTR(account, '@') + 1)) || ',') > 0

    What a hit looks like. Corporate accounts found in third-party breaches. Silence confirms no corporate identities are present in the HIBP snapshot.

  3. Query, enrichment

    Infostealer identity leaks

    Identify corporate users whose credentials were stolen by stealer malware as recorded by Hudson Rock.

    sql, reads hudsonrock_search_by_email
    SELECT email, computer_name, date_compromised, malware_pathFROM hudsonrock_search_by_emailWHERE instr(',' || '{{corporate_domains}}' || ',', ',' || LOWER(SUBSTR(email, INSTR(email, '@') + 1)) || ',') > 0

    What a hit looks like. Corporate accounts compromised by infostealers. Silence means no credentials from these domains were found in the malware database.

  4. Agent triage

    Weigh external exposure risk

    Prioritize exposed identities based on the recency and severity of the leak.

  5. Query, baseline

    Anomalous logins for high-risk identities

    Find successful sign-ins from rare source IP addresses specifically for the corporate identities flagged in the intelligence phase.

    sql, reads hb_auth_signin
    SELECT actor_user_name, src_endpoint_ip, src_location_country, device_hostname, COUNT(*) as login_count, MIN(time) as first_seenFROM hb_auth_signinWHERE status_id = 1  AND ('{{high_risk_identities}}' = '' OR instr(',' || '{{high_risk_identities}}' || ',', ',' || actor_user_name || ',') > 0)  AND ('{{scope_hosts}}' = '' OR instr(',' || '{{scope_hosts}}' || ',', ',' || device_hostname || ',') > 0)  AND time >= datetime('now', '-{{lookback_days}} days')GROUP BY actor_user_name, src_endpoint_ip, src_location_country, device_hostnameHAVING login_count < 5ORDER BY login_count ASC

    What a hit looks like. Successful logins from unusual IPs or locations for high-risk accounts. Silence proves no rare successful authentications occurred for these users.

  6. Query, triage

    Phishing interaction for high-risk users

    Identify web requests containing credential harvesting lures from the compromised users identified earlier.

    sql, reads hb_http_activity
    SELECT device_hostname, actor_user_name, url_hostname, url_path, user_agent, timeFROM hb_http_activityWHERE (LOWER(url_full) LIKE '%login%' OR LOWER(url_full) LIKE '%office%' OR LOWER(url_full) LIKE '%verify%')  AND ('{{high_risk_identities}}' = '' OR instr(',' || '{{high_risk_identities}}' || ',', ',' || actor_user_name || ',') > 0)  AND ('{{scope_hosts}}' = '' OR instr(',' || '{{scope_hosts}}' || ',', ',' || device_hostname || ',') > 0)  AND time >= datetime('now', '-{{lookback_days}} days')

    What a hit looks like. HTTP interaction with potential phishing URLs by compromised users. Silence confirms no such lures were visited by these specific accounts.

  7. Query, enrichment

    Data exfiltration from compromised accounts

    Identify large outbound data transfers originating from the identities previously flagged for high-risk exposure.

    sql, reads hb_network_connection
    SELECT device_hostname, user_name, dst_endpoint_ip, SUM(traffic_bytes) as total_bytesFROM hb_network_connectionWHERE state_kind = 'log'  AND direction = 'outbound'  AND ('{{high_risk_identities}}' = '' OR instr(',' || '{{high_risk_identities}}' || ',', ',' || user_name || ',') > 0)  AND ('{{scope_hosts}}' = '' OR instr(',' || '{{scope_hosts}}' || ',', ',' || device_hostname || ',') > 0)  AND time >= datetime('now', '-{{lookback_days}} days')GROUP BY device_hostname, user_name, dst_endpoint_ipHAVING total_bytes > 50000000ORDER BY total_bytes DESC

    What a hit looks like. Outbound sessions exceeding 50MB originating from high-risk users. Silence means no such data transfers were recorded for these accounts.

  8. Agent triage

    Final correlative analysis

    Determine if the coincidental external exposure matches internal signs of identity theft or follow-on abuse.

  9. Decision

    Route by verdict

    Direct the hunt to containment or manual impact review based on the agent's confidence.

  10. Response action

    Remediate compromised accounts

    Secure the affected identities to stop active abuse immediately.

  11. Analyst task

    Analyst impact review

    Verify the extent of the data impact and check for follow-on persistence.

  12. Analyst task

    Close out and tune

    Document findings and update watchlists for long-term monitoring.

Blind spots

  • Needs SSL decryption for hb_http_activity. Phishing interaction remains unconfirmed without path context; we can see the domain but not whether a credential form was submitted. It would answer: What full URI paths were visited on suspected phishing domains? Remediation: Deploy endpoint-based HTTP inspection or enable corporate proxy SSL decryption.
  • Needs hb_auth_signin with full MFA event details. Inability to distinguish between simple password reuse and sophisticated session hijacking via infostealer logs. It would answer: Was the anomalous login successful because MFA was bypassed or because a session token was stolen? Remediation: Enable and export full conditional access and MFA logs to the centralized log pool.

# Identity Breach and Account Abuse

This phased hunt identifies corporate identities compromised in external breaches and infostealer activity, then monitors those specific accounts for anomalous internal behavior. By correlating external intelligence with authentication, web, and network telemetry, the hunt distinguishes between simple credential exposure and active account weaponization.

## detect-anomalous-logins
<!-- Anomalous logins for high-risk identities -->
Find successful sign-ins from rare source IP addresses specifically for the corporate identities flagged in the intelligence phase.

```sqlite target=identity role=baseline params=(lookback_days=lookback_days, scope_hosts=scope_hosts, high_risk_identities=high_risk_identities)
~~~yaml
baseline:
  compare: first_seen
  window: '{{lookback_days}}d'
expected: Successful logins from unusual IPs or locations for high-risk accounts.
  Silence proves no rare successful authentications occurred for these users.
prevalence:
  by: device_hostname
  key:
  - actor_user_name
  - src_endpoint_ip
  rare_below: 5
reads:
- actor_user_name
- device_hostname
- src_endpoint_ip
- src_location_country
- status_id
- time
silence: not_evidence_of_absence
source: hb_auth_signin
verified: dry-run
verified_at: '2026-10-14'
~~~
SELECT actor_user_name, src_endpoint_ip, src_location_country, device_hostname, COUNT(*) as login_count, MIN(time) as first_seen FROM hb_auth_signin WHERE status_id = 1 AND ('{{high_risk_identities}}' = '' OR instr(',' || '{{high_risk_identities}}' || ',', ',' || actor_user_name || ',') > 0) AND ('{{scope_hosts}}' = '' OR instr(',' || '{{scope_hosts}}' || ',', ',' || device_hostname || ',') > 0) AND time >= datetime('now', '-{{lookback_days}} days') GROUP BY actor_user_name, src_endpoint_ip, src_location_country, device_hostname HAVING login_count < 5 ORDER BY login_count ASC
```
