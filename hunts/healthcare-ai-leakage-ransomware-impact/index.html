Healthcare Data Leakage and Ransomware Impact

Sensitive healthcare data is being leaked via AI chat prompts while ransomware actors prepare or execute high-impact encryption on clinical endpoints, potentially using the same AI interfaces to automate data analysis or extortion.

Brief

Why this hunt?

Proofpoint reports in Healthcare Cyberattacks Hit Harder, Cost More (https://www.proofpoint.com/us/newsroom/news/healthcare-cyberattacks-hit-harder-cost-more) that the financial and operational impact of intrusions in the healthcare sector is rising. This hunt addresses the dual threat of data exfiltration through modern AI tools and the destructive impact of ransomware on clinical environments.

How the hunt flows

The hunt begins by scoping the environment for AI service interactions. The first query searches the hb_http_activity surface for HTTP POST requests directed at common AI domains like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini. This step identifies which hosts are actively sending data outbound, creating a focused list of systems for deeper inspection. Next, the hunt fans out into two parallel queries to find evidence of impact on those specific hosts. One query monitors the hb_file_activity surface for massive spikes in file creation, deletion, or renaming. It filters for processes that touch more than 500 files in a short window, a behavior typical of ransomware encryption. The second parallel query examines the hb_process_activity surface. It looks for rare binaries or processes running from suspicious locations such as temporary folders or public user directories. This identifies potential ransomware droppers or unauthorized exfiltration tools that may not yet have a known signature. Finally, an agent triages the gathered evidence. It correlates the AI network traffic with the file and process anomalies to determine if a host is compromised. If the analyst finds active encryption or massive data leakage, the hunt provides a direct path to isolate the endpoint and begin incident review.

Blind spots

This hunt cannot see the specific text sent in an AI prompt because the HTTP telemetry only captures the hostname and method. Without TLS termination at a proxy, the presence of actual patient data remains an inference. Additionally, many legacy medical imaging and diagnostic systems do not support modern endpoint agents. These machines may undergo encryption without generating the file or process telemetry this hunt requires.

    What a hit looks like. The query returns specific hosts and the AI endpoints they are posting data to; high POST counts suggest frequent data submission. Silence implies no detectable interaction with the listed domains.

  2. Ransomware file activity spikes

    Query · detection candidate

    Find high-frequency file creation, deletion, and renaming events on suspected hosts that typify ransomware encryption.

    reads hb_file_activitysql
    SELECT device_hostname, LOWER(process_name) as process_name_case, COUNT(*) as activity_count, MIN(time) as start_time, MAX(time) as end_time FROM hb_file_activity WHERE activity_id IN (1, 4, 5) AND ('{{scope_hosts}}' = '' OR instr(',' || '{{scope_hosts}}' || ',', ',' || device_hostname || ',') > 0) AND time >= datetime('now', '-{{lookback_days}} days') GROUP BY device_hostname, LOWER(process_name) HAVING activity_count > 500 ORDER BY activity_count DESC

    What a hit looks like. A process on a scoped host touching hundreds of files within a short window indicates potential encryption. Silence suggests no massive file-churn on these hosts.

  3. Suspicious binary prevalence

    Query · baseline

    Identify rare processes or processes running from temporary paths on suspect hosts that may be acting as ransomware droppers or AI data exfiltrators.

    reads hb_process_activitysql
    SELECT LOWER(process_name) as process_name_case, process_path, COUNT(DISTINCT device_hostname) as host_count, MIN(time) as first_seen FROM hb_process_activity WHERE (on_disk = 0 OR LOWER(process_path) LIKE '%\\temp\\%' OR LOWER(process_path) LIKE '%\\users\\public\\%' OR LOWER(process_path) LIKE '/tmp/%') AND ('{{scope_hosts}}' = '' OR instr(',' || '{{scope_hosts}}' || ',', ',' || device_hostname || ',') > 0) AND time >= datetime('now', '-{{lookback_days}} days') GROUP BY LOWER(process_name), process_path HAVING host_count <= 2

    What a hit looks like. Rare binaries or fileless processes running from atypical locations across very few scoped hosts. Silence means no suspicious process anomalies were detected on the scoped set.

  6. Isolate the endpoint

# Healthcare Data Leakage and Ransomware Impact

This hunt identifies two concurrent risks in healthcare environments: the unauthorized submission of patient data to AI chatbots and the behavioral spikes associated with ransomware encryption. It begins by identifying hosts interacting with AI services via HTTP POST requests, then fans out to analyze massive file modification events and rare or detached processes on those same systems. An agent triages these signals to determine if a data leakage incident or an encryption event is in progress on clinician workstations.
