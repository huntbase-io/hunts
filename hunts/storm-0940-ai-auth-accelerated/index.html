Storm-0940 AI-Accelerated Authentication

An adversary is using agentic AI to automate high-volume password spraying and service principal authentication from a known infrastructure range to bypass interactive sign-ins.

Brief

Why this hunt matters

In October 2026, Microsoft published a report titled Storm-0940 uses ARTEX agentic AI ConnectWise supply chain compromise. The research details a campaign where a nation-state actor uses the ARTEX agentic AI framework to compress the attack lifecycle. By automating the transition from high-volume password spraying to the abuse of service principals, the adversary achieves privileged access in hours rather than days. This hunt focuses on identifying that machine-speed pivot.

How the hunt flows

The first phase scopes the environment by identifying domain controllers or identity gateways that have processed traffic from the known Storm-0940 infrastructure. The initial query in hb_auth_signin filters for specific IP ranges reported in the Microsoft intelligence, creating a target list of hosts for deeper investigation.

The hunt then moves into a parallel analysis phase. One query identifies machine-speed password spraying by looking for source IPs with failure rates exceeding 95% and attempt volumes over 500 in a short window. The actor is known to reach speeds of nearly 3,000 attempts per minute. Simultaneously, the hunt monitors hb_cloud_api_activity for sensitive Entra ID operations. It specifically looks for credential additions, role modifications, or consent changes to service principals occurring in the same timeframe.

The final triage phase uses an agent to weigh the evidence. It correlates the source IPs from the high-volume spraying with the source IPs performing the administrative audit changes. If a match occurs, it indicates the AI framework has successfully moved from spraying to active persistence via an application-only authentication flow.

Blind Spots and Limitations

The primary limitation is log retention. Standard Entra ID sign-in logs often expire after 30 days. If the intrusion occurred earlier than the current lookback period, the evidence of the initial spray may be lost. Additionally, the adversary may rotate from their known infrastructure to residential proxies once they achieve initial access. If the subsequent service principal abuse comes from a different IP than the spray, the correlation logic requires manual analyst review to link the activities.

    What a hit looks like. Source IPs with thousands of failures. Storm-0940 activity peaked at 2,900 attempts per minute.

# Storm-0940 AI-Accelerated Authentication

This hunt identifies automated authentication campaigns linked to Storm-0940 and the ARTEX AI framework. It focuses on the machine-speed transition from high-volume password spraying to the abuse of compromised service principal credentials. By correlating authentication volume from identity surfaces with application credential changes in cloud audit logs, the hunt identifies tenants where AI-driven agents have achieved initial access and established persistence.

## high-volume-spraying
<!-- Detect machine-speed password spraying -->
Identify IPs exceeding normal human login rates, focusing on those interacting with the scoped identity infrastructure.

```sqlite target=identity role=baseline params=(scope_hosts=scope_hosts, lookback_days=lookback_days)
~~~yaml
baseline:
  compare: first_seen
  window: '{{lookback_days}}d'
expected: Source IPs with thousands of failures. Storm-0940 activity peaked at 2,900
  attempts per minute.
prevalence:
  by: device_hostname
  key:
  - src_endpoint_ip
  rare_below: 2
reads:
- src_endpoint_ip
- actor_user_name
- status_id
- time
- device_hostname
silence: not_evidence_of_absence
source: hb_auth_signin
verified: dry-run
verified_at: '2026-10-22'

SELECT src_endpoint_ip, COUNT(*) as attempts, COUNT(DISTINCT actor_user_name) as unique_users, SUM(CASE WHEN status_id = 2 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) as failures, MIN(time) as first_seen, MAX(time) as last_seen FROM hb_auth_signin WHERE ('{{scope_hosts}}' = '' OR instr(',' || '{{scope_hosts}}' || ',', ',' || device_hostname || ',') > 0) AND time >= datetime('now', '-{{lookback_days}} days') GROUP BY src_endpoint_ip HAVING attempts > 500 AND (failures * 1.0 / attempts) > 0.95 ORDER BY attempts DESC
```
