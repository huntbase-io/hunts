SafePay: External Access and Identity Consolidation

High severity TLP:CLEAR Part 1 of 3 Research by CISA Published 22 Oct 2026

Hypothesis

An adversary has exploited a known edge vulnerability to gain a foothold and is consolidating control by creating administrative accounts and escalating local privileges via UAC bypass.

Brief

Why this hunt

CISA recently published #StopRansomware: SafePay Ransomware regarding the tactics used by this group. SafePay operators focus on rapid entry through vulnerable edge infrastructure, specifically Cisco VPNs, Veeam backup servers, and SonicWall firewalls. Once inside, they move quickly to solidify their presence by creating administrative accounts. We designed this hunt to find the transition from the initial exploit to the establishment of persistent control.

Hunt Flow

The first phase uses the hb_vulnerability_finding surface to scope the environment. It checks for specific CVEs associated with SafePay, such as CVE-2024-40711 and CVE-2023-20269. This step acts as a gate. If no vulnerable assets report in, the hunt stops. This prevents an analyst from sifting through logs on hosts that were never at risk for this specific entry vector.

Once an exposure is confirmed, the hunt fans out into three parallel behavioral investigations. The first query examines hb_auth_signin for brute-force patterns or anomalous sign-in volumes targeting the exposed hosts. This identifies if the adversary attempts to pivot from an exploit to credential-based access or if they are testing stolen credentials.

The second query monitors hb_account_change for persistence indicators. It looks for new account creations or password resets on critical servers. This behavior often marks the transition from a temporary foothold to a more permanent administrative presence. The hunt looks for activity that deviates from standard administrative windows.

The third query targets privilege escalation via hb_process_activity. It identifies high-integrity processes spawned from interactive shells like cmd.exe or powershell.exe. While these actions occur during legitimate administration, their coincidence with vulnerable edge states increases the likelihood of a UAC bypass.

Finally, an agent synthesizes these signals. It weighs the vulnerability status against the observed behaviors to provide a host-by-host verdict. If the evidence shows both account manipulation and privilege escalation on a vulnerable host, the hunt suggests immediate isolation to stop the adversary before they begin data exfiltration or encryption.

Blind Spots

This hunt depends on the accuracy of the vulnerability inventory. If a firewall or VPN gateway does not report its status to the hb_vulnerability_finding surface, the scoping phase will ignore it. Analysts must ensure all edge devices are correctly enrolled and scanned. Additionally, the hunt may miss account changes if they occur directly on a Domain Controller that lacks an endpoint agent, as it relies on the hb_account_change surface for visibility.

  4. Query, triage

    Brute-force and logon anomalies

    Identify external brute-force attempts targeting administrative or phished accounts.

    sql, reads hb_auth_signin

    What a hit looks like. A high volume of failed sign-ins from a single source IP, potentially targeting multiple accounts.

  5. Query, detection candidate

    Suspicious account manipulation

    Detect the creation of new accounts or unauthorized password resets used for persistence.

    sql, reads hb_account_change

    What a hit looks like. Account creation (activity_id 1) or password changes/resets (3, 4) on critical servers, especially by unexpected actors.

  6. Query, triage

    Local privilege escalation via UAC bypass

    Find high-integrity processes spawned from interactive or shell-based parents, indicating UAC bypass.

    sql, reads hb_process_activity

    What a hit looks like. High integrity processes with command-line arguments that imply interactive shell execution. Silence proves the absence of this specific behavior on the monitored hosts.

    Triage initial access and consolidation

    Synthesize vulnerability state with behavioral evidence to determine if a host is compromised.

  8. Decision

    Route for containment or review

    Trigger host isolation for confirmed intrusions or manual review for ambiguous signals.

  9. Response action

    Isolate compromised host

    Contain the threat before it moves to share enumeration or exfiltration.

  10. Analyst task

    Analyst manual review

    Investigate suspicious findings that did not meet the immediate isolation threshold.

  11. Analyst task

    Hunt close-out

    Document findings and update vulnerability posture.

# SafePay: External Access and Identity Consolidation

SafePay actors use vulnerabilities in Cisco VPNs, Veeam backups, and SonicWall firewalls to enter the network. The hunt identifies exposed assets and monitors for brute-force attacks against the beachhead. The gated structure ensures the analyst only performs expensive behavioral analysis on hosts that show a real exposure. The hunt then checks for new account creations and privilege escalation attempts before an agent weighs the combined evidence and an analyst confirms the threat.
