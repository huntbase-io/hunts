# Hunting Masqueraded svchost and Discovery in N-central RMM Exploitation

### Why Now 10 10Recent reporting by Huntress in their article Critical N-able N-central Vulnerability and Active Exploitation (https://www.huntress.com/blog/n-able-vulnerability-exploitation) highlights how attackers use RMM appliance vulnerabilities to gain a foothold in managed environments. Once the appliance is compromised, the adversary often uses built-in features to move laterally and persist. 10 10### The Hypothesis 10 10An intruder who has compromised an N-central appliance abuses the Take Control feature to drop masqueraded binaries in user folders and perform process enumeration on managed hosts. This behavior mimics administrative activity but leaves distinct traces in endpoint telemetry. 10 10### How the Hunt Flows 10 10The hunt begins by scoping for high-fidelity indicators of defense evasion. The first query searches the hb_process_activity surface for svchost.exe running from non-standard, user-writable paths like Documents or Public folders. While the filename is legitimate, the execution path is not. 10 10Once the initial query identifies potential leads, the hunt fans out into a corroboration phase. This step simultaneously examines two distinct behaviors on the scoped hosts. The first search stack-counts process discovery commands such as tasklist.exe or systeminfo.exe. By filtering for commands seen on five or fewer hosts, the hunt isolates strategic reconnaissance from fleet-wide administrative noise. 10 10The second part of the corroboration phase checks the hb_network_connection surface for traffic to known attacker infrastructure. This step uses a list of reported IPs to provide immediate context to the suspicious processes found earlier. 10 10Finally, the hunt moves to a triage stage. An analyst evaluates the combined evidence of masquerading, reconnaissance, and network connections. This structured approach allows the team to distinguish between a legitimate MSP support session and an active breach. 10 10### Blind Spots 10 10This hunt relies heavily on endpoint visibility. If an adversary lands on a host without a monitoring agent, the masqueraded binaries and local discovery remain invisible. Additionally, the network connection check depends on the retention period of socket activity. If the initial C2 callback occurred before the lookback window, the hunt may only see the local process activity without the corroborating network traffic. 10 10### How to Run This Hunt 10 10We provide this hunt as an open hunt.md playbook. This format is a human-readable and machine-executable document that defines the hypothesis, queries, and logic steps. You can import this file directly into Huntbase or any hunt.md-aware runtime to execute the queries against your telemetry. Because this is a hunt rather than a simple detection, an analyst must perform the final triage and confirm the verdict based on the provided context.
