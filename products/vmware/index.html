    An attacker is established on a vCenter appliance and is using administrative APIs to create local persistence and enable restricted shells for root-level command execution.

    High Rapid7 Part 1 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps CVE-2026-123890

  4. VMware vCenter Authentication Bypass (CVE-2026-123890)

    An adversary has exploited a critical authentication bypass in the VMware Directory Service (vmdir) to gain unauthorized administrative access to vCenter, likely originating from an external or anomalous source IP.

    Critical Rapid7 5 queries, 11 steps CVE-2026-123890, CVE-2026-59309

  5. UAT-12643 Stager Abuse and Registry Persistence

    An adversary is using signed Windows binaries to stage payloads on hosts running targeted software, establishing persistence through masqueraded Edge updater binaries in user-writable paths.

    High Cisco Talos Part 1 of 2 · 4 queries, 11 steps collection, command and control

  6. LiteLLM Supply Chain Poisoning and Persistence

    Critical Unit 42 Part 2 of 2 · 6 queries, 12 steps CVE-2023-27532, CVE-2026-0257

  8. VPN Gateway Exploitation and Unauthorized Authentication

    Critical Unit 42 Part 1 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps CVE-2023-27532, CVE-2026-0257

  9. VerdantBamboo Stolen Credential and Pivot Hunt

    An adversary has used stolen administrative or service account credentials to access edge appliances via VPN or SSH, subsequently using web-based management interfaces to pivot further or deploy persistence.

    High Volexity Part 2 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps command and control, execution

  10. VERDANTBAMBOO Edge Appliance Post-Exploitation

    An adversary compromises an edge appliance, uses an inadvertent sudo configuration to escalate to root, and creates persistence via cron to tunnel traffic through DNS-over-HTTPS.

    High Volexity Part 1 of 2 · 5 queries, 11 steps command and control, execution
