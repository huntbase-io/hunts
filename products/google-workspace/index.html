Threat hunts for Google Workspace

10 hunts covering Google Workspace, each with a hypothesis, the queries that test it and what the hunt cannot see.

10 hunts, newest first

All 506 hunts

  1. ClickFix Post-Exploitation and Payloads

    An adversary has established a beachhead via a ClickFix lure and is now using the finger.exe utility for command-and-control while deploying sideloaded DLLs and infostealers within user-writable directories.

    High Huntress Part 2 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps command and control, defense evasion

  2. ClickFix Paste-and-Run Execution and Persistence

    An intruder has tricked a user into pasting a malicious command into the Windows Run box, causing explorer.exe to spawn an obfuscated shell that establishes persistence via scheduled tasks or Run keys.

    High Huntress Part 1 of 2 · 4 queries, 11 steps command and control, defense evasion

  3. Amazon ClickFix Multi-stage Malware Infection

    An adversary is deploying portable Node.js and IronPython interpreters to user-writable directories to execute obfuscated Doina loaders and Lumma infostealers.

    High Huntress Part 2 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps credential access, defense evasion

  4. Amazon ClickFix Finger Utility Abuse

    An adversary has lured a user to a spoofed Amazon page and pastejacked a command that uses finger.exe to download malicious payloads from a remote domain.

    High Huntress Part 1 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps credential access, defense evasion

  5. ConnectWise M365 Application Abuse

    An adversary has exploited compromised ConnectWise (SkyKick) vendor credentials to access M365 tenants, escalate directory privileges, and persist by adding secrets to secondary applications.

    Critical Huntress 4 queries, 10 steps credential access, initial access

  6. Identity Compromise and SaaS Persistence

    An adversary has bypassed MFA via session hijacking or proxy usage and is establishing persistence through malicious inbox rules or unauthorized OAuth applications in a SaaS environment.

    Medium Huntress Part 2 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps credential access, exfiltration

  7. AI Social Engineering and Ransomware Operations

    An adversary uses an AI-crafted social engineering lure to execute a loader that performs data staging or ransomware encryption.

    Medium Huntress Part 1 of 2 · 4 queries, 11 steps credential access, exfiltration

  8. Identity-Led Cloud Abuse and Secret Discovery

    An attacker has used stolen credentials harvested from unmanaged devices to bypass MFA via push fatigue, subsequently discovering cloud secrets and internal shares to exfiltrate data.

    High Sekoia Part 2 of 2 · 5 queries, 11 steps credential access, impact

  9. External Identity Exposure and Credential Leaks

    Adversaries are using corporate credentials harvested from unmanaged personal devices by infostealers to bypass authentication controls and gain initial access to cloud resources.

    High Sekoia Part 1 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps credential access, impact

  10. ClickFix Browser Injection and Extension Persistence

    An intruder has used a social engineering lure to trick a user into manually injecting a JavaScript loader or installing a malicious Tampermonkey script that facilitates persistent cryptocurrency theft via the Google Visualization API.

    Medium Cisco Talos Part 1 of 2 · 5 queries, 11 steps collection, command and control
