Threat hunts for Exchange

15 hunts covering Exchange, each with a hypothesis, the queries that test it and what the hunt cannot see.

15 hunts, newest first

All 506 hunts

  1. Microsoft 365 Service Health Impersonation and Account Abuse

    An intruder has gained initial access by sending phishing emails that impersonate Microsoft 365 outage notifications, inducing users to click tracking links that lead to credential theft and subsequent unauthorized cloud API activity.

    High Microsoft 3 queries, 10 steps execution, impact

  2. TGR-STA-0049: QUIC C2 and Custom RAT Operations

    An intruder is using HTTP/3 (QUIC) over UDP 443 for command and control and loading custom RAT modules to perform data collection, while masking their activity with synthetic background noise.

    High Unit 42 Part 2 of 2 · 5 queries, 11 steps collection, command and control

  3. TGR-STA-0049 Initial Access via Exploitation and Phishing

    An intruder has established a beachhead by exploiting a public-facing server to drop a web shell or by baiting a user into resolving a lookalike domain associated with the TGR-STA-0049 campaign.

    High Unit 42 Part 1 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps collection, command and control

  4. Turla CerealNeuron Passive Exchange Backdoor

    An adversary has installed a passive .NET backdoor on a Microsoft Exchange server that intercepts commands via specific URI paths within standard Exchange web services.

    Critical ESET Research Part 2 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps collection, command and control

  5. Turla Cereal: Exchange Transport Hijack and .NET Stealth

    An adversary is using sophisticated .NET tradecraft to hijack an Exchange server's mail transport pipeline in memory, using local file markers for command processing and staging encrypted archives of collected emails.

    Critical ESET Research Part 1 of 2 · 5 queries, 11 steps collection, command and control

  6. Endpoint Exploit and Service Crash Monitoring

    An adversary is exploiting unpatched November 2026 vulnerabilities to gain access or elevate privileges, causing service crashes followed by anomalous child processes or suspicious RDP logins.

    High Cisco Talos 5 queries, 11 steps CVE-2026-105501, CVE-2026-105524

  7. Entra ID Identity Hijack and Persistence

    An adversary has replayed session tokens stolen through AiTM relays to register rogue devices and establish persistent MFA methods in the victim tenant.

    High Huntress Part 1 of 2 · 5 queries, 12 steps credential access, defense evasion

  8. ARTEX-Driven Cloud Persistence and Graph Exfiltration

    An adversary is using agentic AI to perform high-velocity reconnaissance, credential additions, and data exfiltration within our Microsoft 365 environment after compromising a trusted application.

    High Microsoft Part 2 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps credential access, exfiltration

  9. Storm-0940 AI-Accelerated Authentication

    An adversary is using agentic AI to automate high-volume password spraying and service principal authentication from a known infrastructure range to bypass interactive sign-ins.

    High Microsoft Part 1 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps credential access, exfiltration

  10. ConnectWise M365 Application Abuse

    An adversary has exploited compromised ConnectWise (SkyKick) vendor credentials to access M365 tenants, escalate directory privileges, and persist by adding secrets to secondary applications.

    Critical Huntress 4 queries, 10 steps credential access, initial access

  11. October 2026 Patch Tuesday Exposure Hunt

    Adversaries are exploiting newly disclosed October 2026 RCE vulnerabilities to trigger anomalous shell execution and deploy payloads on unpatched Windows and Exchange hosts.

    High Cisco Talos 3 queries, 7 steps CVE-2026-100202, CVE-2026-100209

  12. EBurst Password Spraying and Mailbox Exfiltration

    An adversary is conducting automated password spraying via the EBurst tool against Exchange interfaces and then using successful logins to exfiltrate mailbox data via cloud APIs.

    High CISA Part 2 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps CVE-2014-6278, CVE-2015-3306

  13. Perimeter Exploitation and Evasive VPN Persistence

    An adversary exploits vulnerable web services to execute masqueraded payloads and establishes persistence through a renamed VPN client with a unique hash communicating with Integrity Tech infrastructure.

    High CISA Part 1 of 2 · 5 queries, 11 steps CVE-2014-6278, CVE-2015-3306

  14. APT28 Edge Hijacking and AI-Driven Exfiltration

    An adversary has hijacked local DNS settings via compromised edge infrastructure and is using a rare, non-browser process to automate the harvesting of documents for exfiltration via AI APIs or high-port tunnels.

    High Sekoia Part 2 of 2 · 3 queries, 8 steps CVE-2022-38028, CVE-2023-23397

  15. APT28: Outlook and Print Spooler Exploitation

    An adversary is exploiting unpatched Outlook or Print Spooler vulnerabilities to harvest NTLM credentials or escalate privileges, evidenced by outbound SMB connections to external IPs and rare child processes launched by the spooler service.

    High Sekoia Part 1 of 2 · 4 queries, 11 steps CVE-2022-38028, CVE-2023-23397
