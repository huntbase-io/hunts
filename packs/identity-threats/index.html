Identity Threats

Detect and hunt account takeover across Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace, the most common way attackers get in. Schedules detections for MFA fatigue, risky and impossible-travel sign-ins, OAuth consent grants, admin role grants, new application credentials, legacy authentication, suspicious Google logins and domain-wide delegation; checks for admins without MFA; switches on the identity correlation watchers; and brings Hub hunts for token theft, session hijacking, consent phishing and password spraying, with an Identity incident review report template.
